--- Ground-truth observation (C5) ----------------------------------------

The 90.36% confidence figure is NOT an accuracy measurement and is not
reported as one. A single unlabelled image carries no reference label, so
top-1 / top-5 accuracy cannot be computed, and neither can any error
analysis. Obtaining that would require the ImageNet validation split
(50,000 labelled images, registration-gated, licence-restricted) or a
smaller permissively licensed labelled substitute whose terms are verified.

This is the mirror image of the finding on the selected SUT: there, labelled
data was present but drawn from the training split and therefore inadmissible;
here, the evaluation method is sound but no labelled data is present at all.
Both are failures of ground truth rather than failures of software.
