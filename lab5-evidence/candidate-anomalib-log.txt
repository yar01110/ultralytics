MVTec AD is not on this machine. A meaningful anomaly-detection
measurement requires it, because it is what supplies both the image-level
anomaly labels and the pixel-level masks that make Anomalib's AUROC
figures interpretable. Three constraints apply:

  - licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: non-commercial, share-alike, and explicitly
    not to be committed into a fork;
  - download size several gigabytes;
  - obtaining it is a precondition for any quality claim, not an optional
    extra.

Nothing was downloaded during this probe. No anomaly-detection quality
claim is therefore made.

Source: https://www.mvtec.com/company/research/datasets/mvtec-ad
