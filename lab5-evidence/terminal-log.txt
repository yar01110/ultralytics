--- Note on the validation numbers --------------------------------------

These figures are NOT a performance measurement and are not reported as
one. The validation split of coco8 contains 4 images and 17 instances
across 6 classes. Per the dataset header in
ultralytics/cfg/datasets/coco8.yaml, COCO8 consists of the "first 8 images
from COCO train2017" - that is, images drawn from the split the pretrained
weights were trained on. Evaluating on them is evaluating on training data,
which is why mAP50-95 = 0.631 here is far higher than the published
YOLO11n figure for the held-out COCO val2017 set (to be confirmed against
the official model card in Lab #8).

The run therefore establishes that the validation pipeline, the label
loader and the mAP computation all execute correctly - a functional
(plumbing) check. It establishes nothing about detection quality. Lab #8
will repeat this against COCO val2017 (5,000 held-out labelled images) to
obtain a measurement that can support a claim.
